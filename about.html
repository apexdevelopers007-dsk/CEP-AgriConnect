🌱 About AgriConnect

AgriConnect is a simple website created to provide useful agricultural information to farmers in one place.

The website focuses on important problems faced by farmers, such as changing crop prices and changing weather conditions.

👨‍🌾 Importance of Farmers

Farmers play an important role in our society. They grow crops and provide food for people.

  • Farmers provide food for us.
  • They grow different types of crops.
  • They support the agriculture sector.
  • They contribute to the country's economy.

⚠️ Problems Faced by Farmers

  • Changing crop market prices
  • Unpredictable weather conditions
  • Water shortage
  • Crop diseases and pests
  • High cost of farming

💡 Our Purpose

AgriConnect aims to make useful farming information easy to find and understand.

It provides information about crops, market prices, weather, government schemes and farming tips.
