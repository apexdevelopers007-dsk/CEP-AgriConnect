🏛️ Government Schemes

Government schemes can provide financial support, insurance, loans and other benefits to farmers.

AgriConnect provides basic information about some important schemes for farmers.

🌾 PM-KISAN

PM-KISAN is a government scheme that provides income support to eligible farmer families.

🌱 Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana
