🌦️ Weather Information

Weather conditions can change frequently and may affect farming activities and crop growth.

This section provides basic weather information and simple farming advice.

🌤️ Weather Conditions

Weather Effect on Farming Basic Advice
☀️ Sunny High temperature may affect crops Provide proper water
🌧️ Rainy Excess water may affect crops Avoid unnecessary irrigation
☁️ Cloudy Less sunlight Monitor crop conditions
💨 Windy May damage crops Protect weak plants

💡 Weather-Based Farming Tips

  • Check the weather before planning farm activities.
  • Use water carefully during hot weather.
  • Avoid unnecessary irrigation when rain is expected.
  • Protect crops during strong winds.
  • Regularly monitor crop conditions.

⚠️ Important Note

Weather conditions can change quickly. Farmers should check reliable weather sources before making important farming decisions.
